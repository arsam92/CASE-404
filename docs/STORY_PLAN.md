## 1. PREMISE

In **2004**, a fire destroyed the Meridian County Records Archive. A case file numbered **404** was erased from existence — not by the fire, but before it. In the twenty years since, a hidden network has been erasing, altering and stealing the county's records to control legal outcomes. Their tool leaves one signature on every touched file:

```
ARCHIVE SYNC ERROR 404
```

People call the network nothing, because almost nobody knows it exists. Inside itself it is called **The Registry**.

The player is **James Carter**, a newly licensed Special Investigator. He does not know that:

- his father, **Detective Robert Carter**, was the detective who discovered the Registry's founding erasure;
- his father was framed for evidence tampering, erased, and died in disgrace in 2005;
- his own hiring paperwork was filed under a Registry-tagged operation from day one — his training case number, **404-C**, was half arrogance, half a warning from the one man inside who knew.

**The ultimate realization:** *CASE 404 was never missing. It was waiting — for the one investigator who would not stop.*

## 5. THE 23 CASES — ARC BY ARC

### ARC I — Introduction (CASE 001–005): *how the world works*
Teaches every system; builds the core relationships; plants the objects and people that the whole story hangs on.

1. **CASE 001 — The Missing File** *(playable)*. Helen Voss murdered in the Records Annex; file 2019-1183 gone. Bishop convicted — or flees. Plants: **file 1183 → Northbridge**, **ERROR 404 signature**, **torn stub "...404"**, **Kessler's visitor pass**, **Helen's hidden note: "check the fire."**
2. **CASE 002 — The Silent Courier.** Courier put in the hospital; his delivery — burner phone **#8821** — missing. Dispatcher Ruth Ito, passenger Leo Vance. *Plants: #8821, Northbridge dispatch, Leo Vance.*
3. **CASE 003 — The Glass House.** Critic poisoned at the Veridian. **Nina Park** witnesses more than she says. Chef Adrik Sole. *Plants: Nina, the Veridian as a Registry meeting spot.*
4. **CASE 004 — The Inheritance.** Edward Marsh dies; three heirs; the safe holds a pocket watch engraved **R.C.** *Plants: the watch, Marsh's 2004 connection, how the player treats a difficult witness (remembered in 012).*
5. **CASE 005 — The Blue Collar.** Northbridge warehouse death ruled an accident. Foreman Denny Craig. **Sam Okafor "saw nothing."** *Plants: Okafor's hidden log.*

### ARC II — Connections (CASE 006–010): *the links crystallize*
6. **CASE 006 — The Golden Ledger.** Accountant **Marcus Bell** flees, then floats. His ledger: *AEGIS — consulting*, and one circled line item: **client 404**.
7. **CASE 007 — The Paper Trail.** Document storage burns — professionally. **Rhea Voss** enters. *Echoes 2004.*
8. **CASE 008 — The Recurring Number.** An extortion case resurfaces **#8821**. Someone is answering it.
9. **CASE 009 — The Chain of Custody.** Evidence vanishes between two signed transfers. **Marcus Miller** becomes an ally. #8821's owner: an Aegis "consultant" — **Mr. Gray**.
10. **CASE 010 — The Quiet Partners.** The shell web maps out. **DA Hale's first on-page appearance.** First full courtroom duel against **Kessler**.

### ARC III — 404 (CASE 011–013): *the signature*
11. **CASE 011 — The Carter File.** The watch resurfaces in a pawn shop. **Robert Carter** enters the story. Helen's note — "check the fire" — pays off. Personal stakes ignite.
12. **CASE 012 — The Long Memory.** The CASE 004 witness remembers how they were treated. **Bishop resurfaces** (if he fled — or via prison interview if convicted). Marcus is quietly reassigned.
13. **CASE 013 — ERROR 404.** The signature is confirmed on erased files across two decades. Then the player's phone lights up: **"Stop investigating CASE 13."** → *Threat 1–2.*

### ARC IV — Pressure (CASE 014–017): *personal costs*
14. **CASE 014 — The Fall.** **Marcus Miller disappears.** The only witness is **Nina Park** — now a suspect. Protect her or bury her. *Threat 3 & 5.*
15. **CASE 015 — Witness Protection.** Okafor agrees to talk. So does the man sent to stop him. Parking-garage escape (action gameplay).
16. **CASE 016 — The Stolen Truth.** The evidence archive is breached; the case that matters most is stolen back. Recovery operation. *Threat 4.*
17. **CASE 017 — The Northbridge File.** Shipping manifests expose the pipeline. That night, someone is in the player's apartment. *Threat 6.*

### ARC V — Consequences (CASE 018–020): *decisions pay out*
18. **CASE 018 — Cold Coffee.** Sarah is afraid of something she won't name. Marcus's fate surfaces. The Annex becomes a trap (escape gameplay). **Decision: warn Sarah — feeds CASE 021.** *Threat 7.*
19. **CASE 019 — The Turncoat.** Kessler's offer: **accept / refuse / fake-accept.** Hayes's 2004 signature surfaces — handle it with respect or cruelty. Decide who you are.
20. **CASE 020 — The Inheritor.** Okafor's logs prove CASE 005 was murder. **Motion to reopen the 2004 archive fire.** The Registry counterattacks.

### ARC VI — Resolution (CASE 021–023): *one truth*
21. **CASE 021 — The Broken Vow.** **The betrayal.** Sarah — coerced, unless trust and the CASE 018 warning made her the Registry's worst mistake. Hayes may take the bullet. The evidence chain is delivered — or saved.
22. **CASE 022 — FILE 404.** 2004 reconstructed piece by piece: Robert Carter's discovery, the frame job, the fire, Hayes's warning, Wilson's docket. The Registry, fully named.
23. **CASE 023 — The Final Record.** Everything converges in Courtroom 3. The player chooses the name to accuse — **Hale**, **Kessler**, or the wrong target. The endings engine reads the entire record.

## 8. BETRAYAL DESIGN (CASE 021)

**Primary: Sarah Miller.** Her brother's fate is the leverage.
- Trust **below threshold** AND the player ignored her warnings in CASE 018 → the Registry forces her hand: she delivers the evidence chain, and Marcus's location dies with her cooperation. **ENDING D path.**
- Trust **above threshold** AND the player warned her in 018 → she fakes the betrayal, feeds the Registry false copies, and becomes the case that breaks them.

**Secondary: Michael Hayes.** Exposed cruelly in 019 → he goes dark and the 2004 testimony is thinner. Handled with respect → he testifies in 023 — and in one branch takes a bullet meant for the player.

**Record:** every trust-affecting choice from CASE 001 onward feeds these thresholds. Nothing resets between cases.

**Wrong-accusation seeds:** accusing Tomas in CASE 001, burying Nina in 014, or naming Kessler alone in 023 without the Hale chain each mark flags that enable ENDING B.

## 10. CONSISTENCY RULES FOR FUTURE CASES

1. Never contradict the timeline in §3 — 2004 and 2019 are load-bearing.
2. Evidence once discovered stays in the archive; reference it by id (`e1183`, `e404stub`, `e_watch`, `e_thr1`).
3. NPC memory facts (see `state.npc[id].memory`) must be acknowledged in later dialogue (`req: "rel:..."` or flags).
4. Threat levels only rise; `threat>=N` gates are permanent.
5. The Registry never kills carelessly — it erases. Murders happen when someone *reads* the wrong thing.
6. The word "404" may appear as: case number, sync error, ledger line item, room number, pager code — but its meaning is only confirmed in CASE 022.
